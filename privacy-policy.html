Privacy Policy

This page explains what the site does with your information in simple terms. The short version is that the site is mostly static and does not run a sign-up system or personal account area.

Information we collect

We do not ask for personal details through forms on this website. If you contact ToxicBro by email or follow third-party platforms, those services may collect their own information under their own policies.

The site may still use normal hosting logs and browser requests needed to load pages and media.

Third-party platforms

  • YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Discord, Spotify, and similar services manage their own data collection and cookies.
  • Embedded or linked content may behave according to the policy of the platform that hosts it.
  • Please review each service before signing in or sharing personal information.

Cookies and analytics

The website itself is built as a public creator hub. If hosting or platform tools use standard logs or analytics, they are used to keep the site working and to understand general traffic, not to profile visitors personally.

Questions

If you want to ask about this policy, use the official contact page. We will keep the answer direct and human, not buried in legal filler.
